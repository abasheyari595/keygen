Radiological Anatomy – Basic Views (Final)

Upper Respiratory Airway

Pharynx — Lateral

  • Positioning: Standing/sitting lateral; shoulder on bucky; neck gently extended.
  • CR: Perpendicular to the angle of the mandible.
  • Collimation: Skull base → lower neck; nose → behind cervical spine.

Larynx — Lateral

  • Positioning: As above.
  • CR: Perpendicular to the laryngeal cartilage.
  • Collimation: Nose → thoracic inlet; anterior skin → behind cervical spine.

Chest X‑Ray

PA (Postero‑Anterior)

  • Positioning: Chest on bucky; hands on hips; shoulders rolled forward; chin up; cassette top ≈ 2 cm above shoulders.
  • CR: Midline at level of T5 (after deep inspiration; hold breath).
  • Collimation: Apices → costophrenic angles; lateral chest walls included.

Lateral

  • Positioning: Side on bucky; arms raised above head; Left lateral is standard.
  • CR: Mid‑axillary line, centered on bucky.

AP (Antero‑Posterior)

  • Positioning: Back to bucky (or portable/supine if non‑ambulant); hands on waist to move scapulae.
  • CR: Over the sternal angle.

Lordotic View

  • Purpose: To visualize lesions in the right middle lobe or encysted pleural effusion.
  • Positioning: AP position where the patient leans back significantly, so the shoulders touch the bucky while their body is forward.
  • CR: Horizontal to the mid-sternum.

Shoulder

AP

  • Positioning: Back to cassette; arm slightly abducted; hand supinated.
  • CR: Coracoid process. Breath hold.

Axial (Infero‑superior)

  • Positioning: Supine; shoulder at cassette edge; arm abducted (≈ 90° if tolerated).
  • CR: Horizontal into axilla toward humeral head.

Clavicle

AP

  • Positioning: Supine/standing AP.
  • CR: 90° to mid‑clavicle.

PA

  • Positioning: Sitting/standing PA; slight rotation to place clavicle on cassette; head turned away.
  • CR: Mid‑clavicle.

Humerus

AP

  • Positioning: Back of arm on cassette; slight abduction; condyles parallel to table.
  • CR: Mid‑humeral shaft.

Lateral

  • Positioning: Elbow flexed 90°; arm internally rotated; hand on lower abdomen.
  • CR: Mid‑humeral shaft.

Elbow

AP

  • Positioning: Arm on same plane; forearm fully extended; hand supinated.
  • CR: Elbow joint (≈ 2 cm below humeral epicondyles).

Lateral

  • Positioning: Flexed 90°; thumb up (true lateral).

Forearm

AP

  • Positioning: Hand supinated; include wrist and elbow.
  • CR: Middle of forearm.

Lateral

  • Positioning: Elbow 90°; hand lateral; include both joints.
  • CR: Middle of forearm.

Wrist

PA

  • Positioning: Forearm on table; palm down.
  • CR: Dorsum of wrist between styloid processes.

Lateral

  • Positioning: From PA, rotate 90° (ulnar side down).
  • CR: Radial styloid.

Hand

PA

  • Positioning: Palm flat; fingers slightly separated.
  • CR: 3rd metacarpal head.

Lateral

  • Positioning: Ulnar border on cassette (true lateral).

Fingers & Thumb

Fingers — PA & Lateral

  • CR: PIP joint of the finger of interest.

Thumb — AP & Lateral

  • CR: Interphalangeal (IP) joint.

Pelvis & Hip

AP Pelvis (Both Hips)

  • Positioning: Supine; internally rotate feet 15–20° (heels ~20–25 cm apart).
  • CR: Midline, 5 cm above symphysis pubis.
  • Respiration: Suspend on expiration.

AP Hip (Single)

  • Positioning: As AP pelvis (internal rotation).
  • CR: 6 cm below midpoint of line between ASIS and symphysis (femoral neck).

Lateral Hip (Frog‑Leg)

  • Positioning: Supine; flex and abduct affected hip (frog‑leg).
  • CR: Skin crease in inguinal region.

SI Joints, Sacrum & Coccyx

SI Joints — AP (Cephalic) or PA (Caudal)

  • AP: Supine; CR: 5 cm above symphysis with ~25° cephalic angulation.
  • PA: Prone; CR: Between SI joints with ~10° caudal angulation.

Sacrum & Coccyx — AP & Lateral

  • AP Sacrum: Legs flexed; CR: 5 cm above symphysis with ~15° cephalic angle.
  • AP Coccyx: Legs flexed; CR: 5 cm above symphysis with ~10° caudal angle.
  • Lateral: True lateral; CR (Sacrum): ~10 cm posterior to ASIS; CR (Coccyx): over coccyx.

Femur & Knee

Femur — AP & Lateral

  • AP: Supine; leg extended and internally rotated ~15°; CR: Mid‑cassette.
  • Lateral: Side‑lying; knee flexed ~45°; CR: Mid‑cassette.

Knee — AP & Lateral

  • AP: Leg straight; CR: 1 cm below lower pole patella (angle by habitus: thin 5° caudal, average 0°, obese 5° cephalic).
  • Lateral: Knee flexed ~30°; CR: Anterior part of femoral condyle.

Leg (Tibia & Fibula) & Ankle

Leg — AP & Lateral

  • AP: Supine; leg extended (slight medial rotation).
  • Lateral: Side of leg on cassette.
  • CR (both): Mid‑leg.

Ankle — AP & Lateral

  • AP: Supine; knee extended; ankle dorsiflexed to 90°.
  • Lateral: Ankle lateral on cassette; dorsiflexed to 90°.
  • CR (both): On ankle joint (AP: midway between malleoli; Lateral: medial malleolus).

Heel (Calcaneus) & Foot

Calcaneus — Lateral & Axial

  • Lateral: Foot lateral; dorsiflexed 90°; CR: Upper calcaneus.
  • Axial: Dorsiflexed (toes pulled back); CR: ~30° cephalic to plantar surface.

Foot — AP (DP) & Lateral

  • AP (Dorsi‑Plantar): Sole flat on cassette; CR: 90° to tarsal bones.
  • Lateral: Foot lateral on cassette.

Cervical Spine

AP

  • Positioning: Chin forward and up.
  • CR: C4 with ~15–20° cephalic angle.

Lateral

  • Positioning: Shoulders depressed to show C7; chin forward.
  • CR: Horizontal at C3–C4.

Dorsal (Thoracic) Spine

AP

  • Positioning: Supine; legs flexed; expose after deep inspiration.
  • CR: D7 (mid‑sternum) to cassette center.

Lateral

  • Positioning: Lateral; arms above head.
  • CR: Through axilla at D6.

Lumbosacral Spine

AP

  • Positioning: Supine; hips/knees flexed to flatten lumbar lordosis.
  • CR: Midline, ~3 cm above iliac crest.

Lateral

  • Positioning: True lateral; pad under waist to avoid sag.
  • CR: Mid‑axillary line, ~3 cm above iliac crest.
